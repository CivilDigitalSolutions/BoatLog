What BoatLog does

Everything your club needs, in one place

Built around the day-to-day of a rowing club, so committee members and rowers spend less time on admin and more time on the water.

Membership management

Keep member records, roles and renewals up to date. Know who's active, who's a coach, and who still needs to pay their subs.

Boat bookings

Reserve boats and slots without the double-bookings. See what's free, book in seconds, and keep the boathouse running smoothly.

Damage reporting

Log damage the moment it happens. Track repairs, flag boats as out of service, and keep everyone safe on the water.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is BoatLog available yet?

BoatLog is in development. Join the waitlist to be notified as soon as your club can sign up, and to help shape the features that ship first.

Does BoatLog work without WiFi at the boathouse?

Yes. The boat sign-out kiosk is designed to work offline — sign-ins and sign-outs are captured on the device and sync automatically once it's back online. See boat sign-out & kiosk.

What does BoatLog cost?

We haven't published pricing yet, but the plan is simple, honest pricing with no demo-gating. Join the waitlist or get in touch and we'll talk you through it.

Is BoatLog an official British Rowing product?

No. BoatLog is an independent product built by Civil Digital and is not affiliated with or endorsed by British Rowing. It's built around UK clubs' real safety and compliance needs, including British Rowing's own incident reporting process — see incident reporting.
