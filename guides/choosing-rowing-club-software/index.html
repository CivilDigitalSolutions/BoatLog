Choosing rowing club software: a checklist

Before you move your club off paper and spreadsheets, check these ten things.

By Civil Digital, the team building BoatLog · Last updated 23 October 2026

BoatLog is independent software from Civil Digital. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by British Rowing or any organisation named in this guide. Always check the current official guidance and follow your club's own procedures.

The checklist

  1. Works offline at the boathouse, where signal is often poor.
  2. Sign-out and sign-in fast enough that members actually use it.
  3. Safety: a go/no-go flag, overdue alerts and incident logging.
  4. Bookings with clash prevention and fair limits.
  5. Repairs that take a damaged boat out of use.
  6. Memberships and subs, including cash and cheques.
  7. Qualifications such as swim tests and DBS checks, with expiry.
  8. Roles and permissions for captains, coaches and officers.
  9. UK data protection: where data is stored and who can see it.
  10. Cost and set-up time for a volunteer-run club.

FAQ

What is the best software for a rowing club?

It depends on your club. Look for offline sign-out, safety tools, bookings, repairs, memberships and qualifications in one place, with UK data protection.

Does rowing club software need to work offline?

Ideally yes. Boathouses often have poor signal, and a sign-out that fails offline gets skipped.
