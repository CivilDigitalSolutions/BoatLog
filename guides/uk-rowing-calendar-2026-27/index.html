Where did the British Rowing Junior Championships go?

In recent years, junior events have been raced as part of the British Rowing Club Championships rather than as a separate Junior Championships. Check British Rowing's event dates page for the current format.

Heads and weather

Tideway heads are cancelled for strong stream or storms more often than people expect. Fours Head lost two recent editions. Plan for the possibility, and see our guide to rowing in floods and strong stream.

Questions

FAQ

When is Henley Royal Regatta 2027?

Henley Royal Regatta 2027 is published as Tuesday 29 June to Sunday 4 July 2027. Check the regatta's official website for tickets and entries.

When is the Boat Race 2027?

The Boat Race 2027 is on Sunday 11 April 2027. It marks the centenary of the first Women's Boat Race, raced in March 1927, and the running order is reversed for it: the men's race goes first and the women's race takes the later slot.

When is the National Schools' Regatta 2027?

The National Schools' Regatta is published for 28 to 30 May 2027 at Dorney Lake.
