RowSafe 2026: what it is and what clubs need to do

RowSafe is British Rowing's safety guidance. Here is what that means for your club, plus our guides to the topics it covers.

By Civil Digital, the team building BoatLog · Last updated 22 October 2026

BoatLog is independent software from Civil Digital. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by British Rowing or any organisation named in this guide. Always check the current official guidance and follow your club's own procedures.

What RowSafe is

RowSafe is British Rowing's guide to safe practice in rowing. Affiliated clubs commit to it, and it shapes the annual safety audit. It covers club safety organisation, risk assessment, equipment, launches, the water, cold, heat, health and more.

How often it changes

British Rowing normally updates RowSafe once a year, around spring. The 2026 edition is dated February 2026, and British Rowing marks what changed from the previous edition in the document and in a separate list of changes. Read those changes and update your club's safety plan and risk assessments to match.
