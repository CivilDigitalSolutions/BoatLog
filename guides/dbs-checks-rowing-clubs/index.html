DBS checks for rowing clubs: who needs one

A plain-English overview for captains, coaches and welfare officers. Your club welfare officer and British Rowing's safeguarding guidance have the final word.

By Civil Digital, the team building BoatLog · Last updated 22 October 2026

BoatLog is independent software from Civil Digital. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by British Rowing or any organisation named in this guide. Always check the current official guidance and follow your club's own procedures.

Who needs a check

DBS checks are for people whose role involves regular work with children or adults at risk, such as junior coaches, coxing coaches and some volunteers. Not every club member needs one. British Rowing's safeguarding guidance sets out which roles count.

How checks are arranged

In rowing, checks are normally started by the club welfare officer through British Rowing's systems rather than arranged individually. British Rowing's guidance also sets how often checks should be renewed, so expect a renewal cycle rather than a one-off.

What clubs struggle with

  • Knowing who has a current check, and who is waiting for one.
  • Spotting renewals before they lapse.
  • Keeping certificate details private: they are sensitive personal data.

How BoatLog helps

BoatLog keeps a qualifications register where the welfare officer records each DBS check with its date and expiry. Reminders go out before it lapses, and any uploaded evidence is visible only to the roles that need it. BoatLog records the check. It does not carry it out: that still goes through your welfare officer and British Rowing.

Questions

FAQ

Do rowing coaches need a DBS check?

Coaches who work regularly with juniors or adults at risk usually do. British Rowing's safeguarding guidance and your club welfare officer confirm which roles need one.

How often should a DBS check be renewed for rowing?

British Rowing's guidance sets a renewal cycle. Check its current safeguarding pages or ask your club welfare officer.

Can BoatLog do DBS checks?

No. BoatLog records who has a check and when it expires. Checks are arranged through your club welfare officer and British Rowing.
