Qualifications and checks for rowing clubs

Who has passed the swim test, whose DBS check expires next month, and who can drive the launch.

What it does

  • Your own list of qualifications and checks: swim test, capsize drill, DBS check, Powerboat Level 2, first aid, or anything else.
  • Expiry dates and reminders sent 30 days before something lapses.
  • Members add their own, with evidence; a safety officer or admin verifies them.
  • Private evidence: uploaded certificates can only be opened by the roles that need them, and each opening is recorded.

Questions

FAQ

Can BoatLog remind us when a DBS check expires?

Yes. Reminders go out 30 days before a recorded qualification or check expires.

Can members upload their own certificates?

Yes. Their entry stays unverified until a safety officer or admin checks it.

Who can see uploaded DBS certificates?

Only the roles the club needs, such as the safety officer and admins, and every opening is logged.
